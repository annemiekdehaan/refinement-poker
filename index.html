HOW IT WORKS

From idea to estimate.

  1. Start and invite. Share the link or room code with your team.
  2. Discuss the story. The facilitator enters the topic.
  3. Choose your card. Everyone votes independently. ? means unsure, ☕ means break.
  4. Reveal and discuss. Compare the cards and agree on an estimate together.
  5. Save and continue. Export the results as CSV before you leave.

The session runs in the facilitator's browser, which must stay open. Up to 12 people can join. Some corporate networks block direct connections; try another network or contact IT.

Names and votes are shared between participants. PeerJS sets up the connections. There are no accounts, database or analytics. The facilitator temporarily stores the session in this browser tab; close the tab to clear it. Anyone with the room code can join. Use the app for team estimates, not confidential personal information.
